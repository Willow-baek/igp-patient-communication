# Day 2 — Reading & Key Expressions

## Situation

John says he wants to sit more comfortably at work, sleep better and return to his usual walks. He is unsure which goal to focus on first.

## Dialogue

Read silently for meaning first, then read aloud. Focus on clear, natural pacing.

**Physio:** John, what would you most like to get back to doing?

**Patient:** I'd like to sit at work without constantly having to change positions.

**Physio:** How long do you usually need to sit for at work?

**Patient:** Sometimes I have meetings that last about half an hour.

**Physio:** So sitting through a 30-minute meeting more comfortably would be useful?

**Patient:** Yes, definitely.

**Physio:** You also mentioned wanting to get back to running. Is that another priority for you?

**Patient:** Yes, I'd really like to run again, but sitting at work is the main problem at the moment.

**Physio:** That makes sense. Shall we start with the meeting goal as a short-term goal and work towards getting you back to running over time?

**Patient:** Yes, that sounds like a good place to start.
