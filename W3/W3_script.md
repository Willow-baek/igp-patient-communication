# Week 3 — Exploring back pain

John은 약 6주 전부터 허리 통증을 경험하고 있습니다. 오랫동안 앉아 있으면 통증이 심해지고, 일어나서 잠깐 걸으면 조금 나아지는 상황입니다.

대화문을 읽을 때는 단어 하나하나를 외우기보다는 전체적인 대화의 흐름과 질문하는 방식에 집중해 주세요.

## Dialogue

**Physio:** Can you tell me more about what's been happening with your back?

**John:** My back has been feeling achy for a while now.

**Physio:** Okay. Whereabouts in your back do you feel it?

**John:** Mostly in the middle of my lower back.

**Physio:** And when did you first notice it?

**John:** About six weeks ago.

**Physio:** Right. What tends to make it worse?

**John:** Sitting at my desk for too long, especially when I'm working.

**Physio:** I see. Does anything seem to help it settle?

**John:** Getting up and having a bit of a walk usually helps.

**Physio:** And how's it affecting you day to day? Are you finding anything particularly difficult?

**John:** It's making it hard to sit through long meetings at work.

**Physio:** Okay, so sitting for long periods makes it worse, and getting up for a short walk seems to help. It's also affecting how long you can sit at work. Are there any other symptoms?

**John:** Not really. It's mainly just the ache in my lower back.
