## Situation

John Thompson visits a physiotherapy clinic with lower back pain that has been bothering him for approximately six weeks.
He explains that the pain started during a particularly busy period at work. It initially felt mild but has gradually become more noticeable.
He has already seen his GP and received some pain medication, but he has not seen a physiotherapist for this problem before.

## Dialogue

Week 4 — Patient History Dialogue
Physio: Can you tell me how your back pain started?
Patient: It started about six weeks ago during a busy period at work. I was sitting at my desk for long hours, but there wasn't any specific injury.
Physio: Has it changed since then?
Patient: Yes, it's become more noticeable over the last few weeks.
Physio: What makes it worse or better?
Patient: Sitting for too long makes it worse, but getting up and walking around helps a little.
Physio: Have you seen a doctor or received any treatment?
Patient: I saw my GP two weeks ago. They prescribed ibuprofen and advised me to keep moving. I haven't had any scans.
Physio: Do you have any other medical conditions, previous injuries or surgeries?
Patient: No major medical conditions or surgeries. I've occasionally had some neck stiffness.
Physio: Are you taking any medication at the moment?
Patient: Just ibuprofen when the pain gets worse.
Physio: Okay, thank you. That's helpful.
