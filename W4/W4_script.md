## Situation

John Thompson visits a physiotherapy clinic with lower back pain that has been bothering him for approximately six weeks.
He explains that the pain started during a particularly busy period at work. It initially felt mild but has gradually become more noticeable.
He has already seen his GP and received some pain medication, but he has not seen a physiotherapist for this problem before.

## Dialogue

Week 4 — Patient History Dialogue
Physio: Can you tell me how your back pain first started?
John: It started about six weeks ago during a particularly busy period at work. I was sitting at my desk for long hours, but there wasn't any specific injury or incident that triggered it.
Physio: Did the pain come on suddenly, or did it develop gradually?
John: It developed gradually. At first, it was just a mild discomfort, but I started noticing it more frequently over time.
Physio: How has the pain changed over the last six weeks?
John: It's become more noticeable over the last few weeks. Initially, I only felt it occasionally, but now I experience it almost every day.
Physio: Have you experienced anything similar in the past?
John: Not really. I've had occasional back stiffness after sitting for long periods, but I've never experienced this kind of persistent pain before.
Physio: Have you seen a doctor or another healthcare professional about this problem?
John: Yes, I saw my GP about two weeks ago because the pain wasn't improving.
Physio: Did your GP recommend any treatment or further investigations?
John: They prescribed ibuprofen and advised me to keep moving and avoid prolonged bed rest. I haven't had any scans or other investigations.
Physio: Do you have any other medical conditions that we should be aware of?
John: No, I don't have any major medical conditions.
Physio: Have you had any previous injuries, hospitalisations or surgeries?
John: No previous major injuries or surgeries. I've occasionally experienced some neck stiffness, but nothing that required medical treatment.
Physio: Are you currently taking any medications or supplements?
John: Just ibuprofen when the pain gets worse. I'm not taking any other regular medication.
Physio: Okay, thank you. That's really helpful. It gives me a better understanding of your medical history and how your symptoms have developed.
